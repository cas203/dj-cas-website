The Story

DJ CAS

Eric Casavant, also known as CAS, fell in love with music at a very young age. Growing up in Waterbury, Connecticut, CAS built a name for having great music taste and developed his natural grasp for music into a career as a DJ and producer.

CAS delivers high-energy, crowd-moving performances across open format, hip hop and house, with experience performing and hosting events internationally.
