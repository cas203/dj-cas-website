The Story

DJ CAS

The story of DJ CAS is one of persistence. Growing up in Waterbury, Connecticut, Eric Casavant developed a passion for music at an early age.

CAS is a professional DJ and music producer with over a decade of experience delivering high-energy, crowd-moving performances. Known for his versatility and sharp ear for music, he blends hip-hop, open format, electronic and Top 40 sounds to create unforgettable experiences.
